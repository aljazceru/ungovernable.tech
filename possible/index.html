What's possible

A map of what you can actually do today by combining the technologies in this library. Each capability is a real goal; the steps under it are working building blocks, each linking to its entry. Nothing here is theoretical unless its readiness says so.

compose → don't trust

People often do not act on freedom technology because they do not know it exists, or cannot see how the pieces fit together. This page is the opposite of a product pitch: it starts from things you might want to do, and shows the primitives — already documented here — that compose into each one. Filter by pillar, or just explore.
