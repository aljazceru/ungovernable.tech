Web of Trust Thursday — July 02 2026 Weekly Update

published 2026-07-02 3 min read Identity, Social and Trust

DATED BRIEFING // 2026-07-02 // may be outdated

This page reflects what was known on 2026-07-02. It is kept as a record, not maintained as a reference; current knowledge lives in the pillar entries.

TL;DR — This Week

  • Nostr WoT ecosystem acceleration: nostr-wot GitHub org releases extension v0.3.0 (with Lightning wallet/NWC), JavaScript SDK for trust queries/distance scoring, and core toolkit for graph crawling/visualization.
  • Web of Trust Foundation: Netherlands-based non-profit advancing open Nostr-based WoT protocol for moderation and discovery; board includes long-time Nostr contributors.
  • NIP-85 Trusted Assertions in production: Live providers like wot.klabo.world (PageRank-based, kinds 30382–30385) crawling follow graphs, publishing signed scores, integrating mute lists and community signals. Auto-re-crawl every 6 hours.
  • Browser extension & portability: Leon Acosta's Nostr WoT extension enables portable trust networks across apps/clients; emphasizes following your graph everywhere.
  • Client & ecosystem adoption: Continued mentions in Nostr News, client updates (Amethyst, Primal), OpenSats funding contexts; visual demos and explanations proliferating.
  • OpenPGP side: RFC 9580 (July 2024) remains the standard; discussions around v6 keys, Sequoia-PGP, and WoT compatibility persist but no major new protocol shifts this week.

Freedom-tech takeaway: Nostr's graph-native WoT (follows, mutes, zaps, NIP-85 oracles) is hardening into a practical anti-spam and reputation primitive. Combined with extensions and oracles, it delivers composable, user-controlled trust without recreating centralized moderation. Cross-pollinates with GPG-style certification for hybrid identity use cases.

The Week in Detail

Nostr WoT Infrastructure Maturation

  • nostr-wot organization (github.com/nostr-wot): New public repos including nostr-wot-extension (browser extension for portable WoT), nostr-wot-sdk (JS SDK for distance queries, membership checks, trust scores), and nostr-wot toolkit for social graph analysis.
  • Extension v0.3.0 adds built-in Lightning wallet, NWC, WebLN integration.
  • Focus on making WoT follow the user across clients rather than rebuilding per-app.
  • Web of Trust Foundation (weboftrustfoundation.com): Launched to build open Nostr-based protocol for human-trust moderation and discovery. Governed by Nostr veterans; active at conferences and with developers.
  • Live scoring oracles:
  • wot.klabo.world (joelklabo/wot-scoring): PageRank crawler publishing NIP-85 events (user/event/addressable assertions). Consumes kind 10040 authorizations, NIP-51 mutes for community signals, detects trust communities via label propagation. Re-crawls every 6 hours. Enriches with trustedrelays.xyz data.
  • Educational & visual resources: Updated explanations (freakoverse.github.io/wotonnostr), HackerNoon deep-dive on extension motivations, nostrcompass.org coverage, and nostr-wot.com/blog posts on scoring algorithms (0-1 trust scores based on distance/mutual follows).

NIP-85 Trusted Assertions & Real-World Use

  • NIP-85 enables offloading heavy graph computations to trusted providers who publish signed assertions. Now seeing multiple independent providers and composite scoring.
  • Use cases: Relay spam filtering, client ranking of authors/events, marketplace reputation, community moderation without central authority.
  • Trade-off highlighted: Local graphs preserve autonomy but require compute; global/provider scores are convenient but introduce oracle trust assumptions. Best practice remains explicit policy (hop distance, freshness, context).

OpenPGP WoT & Broader Context

  • No major new developments this week; ecosystem continues migration toward RFC 9580 (v6 keys, modern algorithms) and abuse-resistant discovery (WKD, keys.openpgp.org).
  • Ongoing discussions around WoT in v6 context (some legacy signature handling changes) and alternatives like Sequoia-PGP.
  • Continued relevance as historical foundation and for software signing / email use cases that complement Nostr social graphs.
