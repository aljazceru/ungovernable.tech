Akash — Mainnet 18 Activated June 11, BME Burn Rate Accelerating

Mainnet 18 Upgrade — June 11, 2026

Akash Network's Mainnet 18 upgrade activated at block #27,230,465 on June 11, 2026 at approximately 14:00 UTC, introducing two production features:

AEP-82 — Resource Reclamation (Completed June 11) Previously, when a provider terminated a lease, the only option was immediate closure — leaving tenant workloads with no graceful shutdown window. Resource Reclamation adds a structured exit mechanism: - Providers can signal intent to close a lease within a defined grace period - Tenants receive advance notice and can migrate workloads before termination - On-chain escrow protects deposits during the transition - Eliminates abrupt data loss from provider-side terminations

Oracle v2 — Wall-Clock Timestamps Replaces block-height references for staleness detection, TWAP calculations, and price queries. Block-height-based staleness is fragile when block times vary; wall-clock timestamps provide deterministic oracle behavior independent of chain throughput.

Market Order Close Fix — corrects edge cases in closing market orders.

BME Burn Momentum

From on-chain data as of May 26: 500,000 AKT burned via BME. The burn rate has continued through mid-June as monthly compute spend holds above the $5M Q1 2026 milestone. The BME mechanism (live March 23, 2026) is the first major test of tokenomics-driven deflation in a DePIN compute network at this scale.

AKT price context (mid-June 2026): approximately $0.85–$0.97 range, representing a ~25% weekly gain in late May as top AI/DePIN performer. The AKT/ACT (stablecoin output from BME) dual-token model is functioning as designed: providers receive stable ACT, tenants burn AKT, speculation is separated from settlement.

GPU Waitlist Expansion

Akash's GPU inventory continues growing through Q2 2026: - H200 nodes actively deployed on mainnet - B200 nodes added to the GPU waitlist as provider demand for Blackwell-class hardware grows - Consumer GPU tier: RTX 4090 and RTX 5090 nodes supported via Homenode (live May 2026) - AkashML continues processing high-volume AI inference tokens via OpenRouter

Confidential Computing Path — AEP-65 Live, DOOR SDK Progress

AEP-65 Confidential Computing with TEE went live May 19, 2026. The community DOOOR SDK project (github.com/akash-network/discussions/872) continues developing the integration layer for TEE attestation into Akash workloads. AEP-29 (Hardware Verification using Trusted Execution) provides provider-side hardware attestation live on-chain.

The combination of AEP-65 (confidential workloads), AEP-29 (provider attestation), and BME (stable pricing) positions Akash as a full-spectrum decentralized cloud — from open containers to TEE-isolated confidential compute.

Fluence — Instant GPU Access Live, FLT Grid League

GPU Containers and VMs Now Instant-Access

Fluence announced (via X/@fluence_project, June 2026) that GPU Containers, Virtual Machines, and Bare Metal deployments are now available for instant access via the Fluence Console — no waitlist, no gating. This is a significant UX step from the previous gated beta.

The Fluence Console now provides: - GPU Containers: isolated GPU-accelerated workloads in managed containers - Virtual Machines: full VM deployments for more complex workloads - Bare Metal: dedicated hardware for high-performance or latency-sensitive jobs - USDC stablecoin pricing (no crypto volatility for enterprise bills) - Arbitrum Orbit L2 fee structure (low gas vs. Ethereum L1)

FLT Grid League on Gate

Fluence launched a FLT Grid League competition on Gate.io, rewarding traders who generate volume in the FLT-USDT pair with a share of 3,000 USDC in prizes. The competition ties speculative trading activity to FLT liquidity and network awareness — a DePIN-adjacent token incentive pattern.

DAO Governance Migration

Fluence DAO governance has migrated from Tally to the native Token App (flt.fluence.network/governance). All governance functions — voting, proposal creation, DAO updates — are now accessible natively within the Fluence ecosystem. Tally's shutdown for many protocols makes native token governance the canonical path.

SuperAI Conference Presence

Fluence exhibited at SuperAI Conference (Singapore, June 2026), demonstrating GPU availability from the Fluence network alongside io.net. The booth showcased Fluence's global GPU cluster network, available locations, and the new GPU Auction mechanism for price discovery on compute capacity.

Render Network — AI Compute Subnet Expansion, AI Sector Tailwind

Dispersed.com GPU Marketplace Growth

Render's dedicated AI compute subnet (Dispersed.com, launched Solana Breakpoint 2025) continues expanding its GPU marketplace: - GPU marketplace grew 87% year-over-year (reported March 2026) - Rendering compute power grew 40% year-over-year - New governance proposal for additional compute subnets approved at RenderCon 2026 (March 2026)

The network's pivot from "rendering-only" to "rendering + AI compute" is reflected in the dual-track growth metrics — rendering remains the established revenue base, while AI inference and training represent the growth vector.

Token Rebrand Complete

The RNDR → RENDER token rebrand (Solana SPL token) is complete. Both names appear in wild; market data providers have migrated to RENDER ticker. The rebrand aligns the project name with the network brand.

AI Crypto Sector Rotation

RENDER benefited from capital rotating into AI-linked crypto assets in 2025-2026, alongside AKT's ~72% YTD surge. The DePIN compute sector broadly saw increased market attention as AI infrastructure demand grew. Render's 9-year track record (founded 2017) provides a credibility argument vs. newer entrants.

Aethir — Enterprise GPU Cloud, DePIN Integration Expansion

Strategic Compute Reserve Model Maturing

Aethir's ATH Strategic Compute Reserve model — where enterprises and funds commit to GPU capacity purchases before hardware is deployed — continues attracting institutional participants. The landmark $260M Axe Compute (NASDAQ: AGPU) deal (2,304 NVIDIA B300 Blackwell GPUs) announced in early June established the model at institutional scale.

Ongoing developments: - Enterprise AI and robotics clients added to the customer roster - ATH token settlement and staking mechanics operating at scale - H2 2026 roadmap includes agentic AI booking real-time GPU inference — autonomous AI agents that dynamically provision GPU capacity

AI Compute Panel — June 2, 2026

Aethir, Nosana, Akash, and io.net participated in a joint panel "Centralized vs. Decentralized AI Compute" on June 2, 2026. The panel covered: - Competitive positioning of decentralized GPU networks vs. hyperscalers - GPU demand/supply imbalance and DePIN's structural role - Enterprise onboarding pathways for Fortune 500 companies - Potential cross-network collaborations

This was the first public event putting all four major DePIN GPU networks on the same stage, reinforcing decentralized compute as a coherent sector rather than isolated projects.

io.net — Dell Partnership, DePIN Hub Integrations

Dell Technologies Authorized Partner Status

io.net's partnership with Dell Technologies (announced early June 2026) has moved into the Authorized Partner and Cloud Service Provider program — a formal channel relationship that gives io.net access to Dell's enterprise sales motion. This is a supply-side play:

  • Dell's enterprise customers can now be directed toward decentralized GPU capacity
  • io.net gains credibility as a supplier in Dell's hardware ecosystem
  • Co-marketing and demand generation between the two companies

DePIN Hub Integrations

io.net's June 2026 integrations on DePIN Hub: - Creator.Bid: image model scaling on decentralized GPUs - Alpha Network: secure decentralized AI/Web3 environment - Mira Network: strategic AI landscape partnership

These partnerships represent io.net's positioning as a GPU infrastructure layer that other AI/Web3 projects build on top of, rather than a direct-to-consumer product.

Production Case Studies

From io.net's published metrics: - Wondera (AI music): $2.48M savings vs AWS equivalent, 552,000 GPU hours, 96 GPUs (64 H100s + 32 H200s) - Leonardo.Ai: scaled from 14K to 19M users, 50%+ cost reduction vs AWS - Platform: 30,000+ GPUs claimed, up to 70% lower cost than AWS

Sector Context — Mid-June 2026

GPU Supply/Demand Dynamics

  • NVIDIA H100 at ~$7.90+/hour on AWS vs. 40-70% discounts on decentralized alternatives
  • xAI Colossus at 555,000 GPUs (January 2026), roadmap to 1M — illustrating hyperscaler concentration
  • Decentralized GPU networks collectively represent a small but growing counterweight
  • AI agents consuming 10-40x more tokens than human-to-AI interaction (Greg Osuri's energy constraint argument)

DePIN Compute Revenue

  • $200M+ annualized protocol revenue across leading DePIN GPU networks (early 2026)
  • On-chain revenue aggregating ~$150M/month across DePIN networks broadly
  • First non-crypto-native enterprise contracts (Axe Compute, Predictive Oncology) demonstrating real-market adoption

Regulatory and Compliance

  • SOC2 and GDPR compliance achieved by io.net — enterprise procurement requirements being addressed
  • DePIN compute projects increasingly pursuing enterprise-ready compliance posture
