#### P2P Exchanges
- [Haveno Reto](https://github.com/retoaccess1/haveno-reto) - Community Haveno network distribution for Monero-and-Tor-based P2P exchange
- [Bisq](https://bisq.network/) - Decentralized P2P Bitcoin exchange network using Tor and local payment methods
- [Bisq Whitepaper](https://docs.bisq.network/exchange/whitepaper.html) - Architecture and threat-model overview for Bisq's Tor-based P2P exchange
- [Haveno](https://github.com/haveno-dex/haveno) - Monero-and-Tor-based decentralized exchange platform for crypto and fiat trades
- [BasicSwap DEX](https://github.com/basicswap/basicswap/) - Atomic-swap DEX with decentralized order book, no accounts, and no trading fees
- [RoboSats](https://github.com/RoboSats/robosats) - Private Lightning-based P2P Bitcoin exchange using Tor and hold invoices
- [DCRDEX](https://github.com/decred/dcrdex) - Non-custodial atomic-swap exchange with no KYC and no trading fees

#### Private Marketplaces
- [OpenBazaar](https://openbazaar.org/) - Decentralized marketplace project planning a 3.0 relaunch after the original shutdown
- [OpenBazaar Haven](https://github.com/OpenBazaar/haven) - Mobile app for the original OpenBazaar decentralized marketplace network
- [Particl Desktop](https://github.com/particl/particl-desktop) - GUI for Particl Marketplace and PART wallet focused on private peer-to-peer commerce
- [Particl Core](https://github.com/particl/particl-core) - Privacy coin and platform backend for Particl's decentralized marketplace ecosystem
