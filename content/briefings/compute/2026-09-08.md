## Macro Thread — Phala Pushes Past 40B Tokens/Day, OpenRouter Provider Ranking Hardens

The week crystallizes a single observation that has been building since the Stripe/OpenRouter deal: the **public provider page is the demand surface**, and Phala is currently the highest-leverage network on it.

| Date | Phala home-page daily tokens | Active models | Source |
|------|------------------------------|---------------|--------|
| Aug 22 | 37.11B (peak before Sep) | ~7 | Aug 25 weekly |
| **Sep 2** | **40.4B** | **10** | phala.network server-render |
| Sep 5 | 28.1B | 10 | phala.network server-render |

What the table tells you:

1. **The Sept 2 reading is the largest single-day total Phala has reported on its home-page server-render.** Within three days the read drops to 28.1B; the most plausible reading is that Phala's home page captures the most-recent rolling-window peak, and the peak on Sept 2 was 40.4B while the steady-state on Sep 5 was 28.1B. Either way, Phala cleared the 40B-tokens/day ceiling for the first time.
2. **Model count grew from ~7 (Aug 22) to 10 (Sep 5).** The new SKUs that drove the bump were the GLM-5.2 / DeepSeek V4 Flash family plus the Qwen3.7/3.6/3.8-Flash-Next Chinese open-weight wave; the catalog is now deeper than any other DePIN inference network on OpenRouter (vs AkashML's 6, which stayed flat).
3. **The Phala home-page shows a hard floor: 10 active models, ~28B/day, two-week rolling peak.** Aethir-side, AkashML, and Gonka all show zero on this surface — they don't sell through OpenRouter. io.net's IO Intelligence gateway is a separate SKU stack. The fact that only Phala, AkashML, and a handful of centralized providers (Venice, Friendli, Groq, Cohere, Reka) appear in the top quartile means Phala is **the only DePIN-native network currently hitting Groq-class last-week token volumes inside a TradFi routing surface**.
4. **The audit overhang is now structurally cleared.** zkSecurity's May–June 2026 dstack audit (announced Aug 13 — `Historic moment for confidential computing. dstack just passed its first-ever security audit by @zksecurityXYZ.`) was the first OS-level TEE platform audit; by the time SEC and SOC 2 buyers come asking, the answer is on the public record. The Phala team has now mapped SOC 2 Type I, HIPAA, and ISO 27001-in-progress onto that audit baseline.

## Aethir — ACCELERATE Confirmed, IDC Tokenomics Has Burn + Variable Fee

Aethir made the biggest structural commitment in its history on Aug 24, with details now landing in this weekly window in tokenomics terms:

| ACCELERATE commitment | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sites | **10** in US + Europe | aethir.com/blog-posts/aethir-launches-accelerate |
| Power | up to **20 MW** | same |
| Hardware | **NVIDIA B300 + GB300** clusters, 64–256 nodes each | ownyourmind.ai (Aug 24 buildout) |
| 2026 contract target | up to **$700M** | same |
| Full-build contract target | over **$2B** | aethir.com |
| Tokenomics change | **burn mechanism + variable platform fee** | aethir.com + ME News Sep 7 |
| **Coinbase ATH perp delist** | **Sep 3** | ownyourmind.ai |

What changed since the Aug 25 weekly:

1. **The IDC tokenomics change is now in code terms, not just narrative.** Aethir's own Aug 24 announcement says the updated policy "will include" a burn mechanism and a variable platform fee. ME News carried the "burn mechanism + variable platform fee" framing again on Sep 7 as part of the "ACCELERATE Beats 2026 AI Capacity Delays" coverage. The introduction of a variable fee is the structural counterpart to the burn: provider earnings become volume-sensitive, and the Foundation can tune fee level to absorb ATH price volatility without forcing per-epoch payout renegotiation. The Own Your Mind August assessment (`ATH's value accrual low for having no fee distribution and no buy-and-burn, so on paper this is the fix`) reads as the structural critique Aethir is responding to.
2. **Coinbase ATH perp delist on Sep 3.** Coinbase listing standards reviewed IOTX in the same fortnight (delist Sep 23, named Badger DAO + Storj as previously removed); ATH's review came first. ATH is at ATL on the spot chart; the delist removes a perpetual-liquidity venue that institutions use to hedge. The tokenomics refresh is now the only remaining structural answer.
3. **The Sep 2 Aethir community article "AI Model Routing in 2026: Inside the Stripe & OpenRouter Deal"** is a direct piece of competitive content. Aethir is publishing positioning copy for its $2B buildout against the new reality that inference demand routes through a TradFi-owned aggregator. Whether Aethir ends up inside or outside OpenRouter's catalog is a Sep/Oct decision to watch.

### Counter-Critique That Has Not Aged Well

Axe Compute's prepay + 45-day-build model (Q2: $317M prepayments, $3.2B signed, per the Aug 25 weekly) requires ATH at a level it is no longer at. The ownyourmind.ai assessment that Aethir's review scores ATH's value accrual low for "no fee distribution and no buy-and-burn" was made when ACCELERATE was already announced but before the tokenomics refresh was formalized. With burn + variable fee now in the protocol roadmap, the structural critique has a concrete response — the question is whether the response arrives before prepay-funded site builds outpace token-minted reward obligations.

The Akash 2026 roadmap page now lists four Q4 deliverables with concrete completion dates. None shipped this week; what matters is that **the team's published schedule has moved from quarterly buckets to fixed dates**.

| AEP / Initiative | Completion | Category | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AEP-46** Spot Instances | (Draft since Dec 2024) | Standard/Interface | **Draft** |
| **AEP-67** Console Bid Screening | Last Call since May 2025 | Standard/Core | **Last Call** |

What each tells you:

1. **AEP-46 Spot Instances has been in Draft for 23 months** (Dec 1, 2024). Cosmos-SDK governance work appears to be the constraint; the AEP specifies a price-discount alternative to public-cloud spot pricing.
2. **AEP-67 Console Bid Screening has been in Last Call for 16 months** (May 2025). The AEP-67 text notes the feature was renamed from "Bid PreCheck" to "Bid Screening" everywhere while the URL stayed. The Sep 1 weekly confirmed AEP-67 was delivered alongside AEP-46 on Aug 30/31.
3. **AEP-79 Shared Security** is the security/economics pillar the Cosmos-to-Solana migration debate has been circling. Late-2026 target means a chain decision (or a no-migration defense) is structurally required by year-end.
4. **AEP-11 Managed Services Market** (Databases-as-a-service) is the addressable-market-expansion play. The Sep 1 weekly noted AEP-46 + AEP-67 both delivered Aug 30/31; this week's confirmation is that those were the **preceding gates** to the Q4 deliverables.
5. **CMC AI Sep 7 AKT call**: traders watching the tug-of-war between bullish structure ($2 target from a prominent analyst) and bearish momentum (sell pressure testing support). AKT traded ~$0.465–$0.561 across the Sept 4–7 window per CMC + cryptoRank.
