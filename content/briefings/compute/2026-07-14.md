## Render Network — Negative Supply, Dispersed Expansion, Messari Deep Dive

Render crossed into structural GPU shortage territory in Q2 2026, with demand outstripping available nodes for the first time in the network's history. The June 25 Crypto Briefing report highlighted onboarding of ~60,000 new GPUs across 180 countries in six months, all immediately utilized.

### Key July Developments
- **Messari "Understanding Dispersed" report (July 7-8 2026)**: Frames Render's decentralized GPU network (via the Dispersed compute subnet) as a direct response to global AI compute shortages. Positions the protocol's distributed model against centralized hyperscaler constraints (power, zoning, latency).
- **Dispersed Windows client expansion (mid-2026 rollout ongoing)**: Extends node onboarding to the massive Windows user base, lowering barriers for consumer-grade GPUs to contribute to AI inference and rendering workloads.
- **Salad Network integration (April 2026 effects persisting)**: Continues to feed ~60k GPUs into the network with payments routed through the token burn mechanism.
- **Foundation June 2026 report**: Covers subnets explainer and ecosystem compute networks; cumulative burns tied to usage continue rising.

Render's valuation-to-revenue gap remains wide (~400x in prior analysis), but real utilization metrics (negative supply) signal product-market fit in the AI inference boom. See [Render Network](/kb/render-network/) for full project overview.

## Phala Network — Confidential AI Scale & Academic Presence

Phala continues to lead in TEE-protected confidential compute, reporting **4.4B confidential model tokens/day** (as of July 12, 2026) across 10 active models on its OpenRouter provider integration.

### Recent Highlights
- **ICML 2026 presentation (July 10)**: Phala presenting "legal bench paper" on confidential AI and TEE verifiability — important academic validation for the privacy-preserving inference thesis.
- **Platform momentum**: Phala Cloud delivers private LLM inference, sealed agents, and verifiable compute on Intel TDX + NVIDIA H100/H200. L2 migration to Ethereum (Nov 2025) enabling better developer access and scalability.
- **Model additions**: Recent uncensored/open models (Qwen3.6 35B-A3B, Gemma-4 26B "Heretic") live on the inference platform, emphasizing refusal-free, high-utility deployments.
- **Metrics context**: Earlier end-2025 figures (2,529 CVMs, 3.05B tokens/day peak) have scaled further; partnerships (Venice AI, ai16z/Eliza, Neurochain) continue to drive workloads.

Phala exemplifies the [Confidential Computing](/kb/confidential-computing/) + decentralized GPU intersection. See [Phala and 0G](/kb/phala-and-0g/) and related TEE files for deeper dives. Competitive edge: on-chain attestation verifiable by smart contracts, no trust in node operators.

## Akash Network — Tooling Releases, Sustained AI Demand

Incremental but steady progress. Akash provider daemon **v0.14.2** released July 2, 2026, with fixes around leased IP inventory exposure in status responses. Node v2.1.0 (June 8) remains current.

### Ongoing Context from Q1/Q2
- BME (Burn-Mint Equilibrium) fully operational since March 23, 2026; every compute workload drives AKT market activity.
- Homenode Early Access and Akash Agents driving broader supply (consumer GPUs) and easier tenant onboarding for agentic workloads.
- Messari Q1 2026 report (May/June coverage) noted provider contraction (58 active) but highlighted named tenants (Venice, ElizaOS, Gensyn, etc.) and $5M monthly spend milestone crossed earlier in 2026.
- Solana migration RFP remains open discussion point (late 2026 target floated in June); no new governance votes as of mid-July.

Akash's reverse-auction model and Kubernetes-native approach keep it positioned for general workloads beyond pure rendering/inference. Confidential Computing (AEP-65 TEE target July 2026) remains a key upcoming milestone. See [Akash](/kb/akash/), [Aethir](/kb/aethir/), and [Bittensor](/kb/bittensor/) for related DePIN compute plays.

**Trade-offs summary**: Raw GPU marketplaces (Render, io.net, Akash Homenode) excel at price discovery and scale but expose data to providers.
