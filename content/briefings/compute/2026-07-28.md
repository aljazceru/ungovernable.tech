Render's dual-use model (rendering + AI inference) and direct token utility in OTOY products strengthen its position. See [Render Network](/kb/render-network/) for full project overview. Valuation-to-revenue gap narrowing with proven product-market fit in the inference boom.

**Trade-offs summary**: Raw GPU marketplaces (Render Dispersed, Akash Homenode, io.net) deliver price discovery and massive scale but expose data unless TEE-layered. Confidential TEE approaches (Phala, upcoming Akash AEP-65) add verifiable privacy with modest overhead. Research/distributed RL (Prime Intellect) trades immediate marketplace simplicity for long-term open model sovereignty. Compute-over-data and proof-based systems offer alternative trust models. DePIN networks consistently demonstrate significant cost advantages (50-70% below AWS) for inference. See [Overview - Decentralized Compute](/kb/decentralized-compute/) for full taxonomy.

## Freedom-Tech Takeaways & Recommendations

1. **Layered Strategy**: Use raw marketplaces (Render/Akash) for cost-sensitive, non-sensitive workloads. Layer confidential TEE (Phala) for private data/AI agents. Monitor Prime Intellect for open distributed training primitives.

3. **Self-Host & Verify**: Run local nodes where feasible; demand on-chain attestation for confidential workloads. Test Homenode for personal GPU contribution.
4. **Real-World Signals**: Enterprise pilots (Salad), exchange listings, direct payment integrations, and massive funding rounds indicate maturing product-market fit beyond crypto speculation.
5. **Next Steps**: Track Akash AEP-65 launch; monitor Render supply/demand post-Coinbase/OTOY; follow Prime Intellect open releases; benchmark inference costs across networks vs centralized baselines.
