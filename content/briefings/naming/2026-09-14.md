### 2. Root KSK rollover — T-27 days, Reveal Day date still pending

**T-27 days to Oct 11, 2026 KSK-2024 cutover**; old-key retirement Jan 2027. ICANN's Aug 11 guidance document ["What to Expect During the Root KSK Rollover"](https://www.icann.org/en/system/files/files/ksk-rollover-expect-27jul26-en.pdf) is the primary reference; ICANN blog Jul 27 ["Preparing for the Root Zone KSK Rollover: What You Need to Know"](https://www.icann.org/en/blogs/details/preparing-for-the-root-zone-ksk-rollover-what-you-need-to-know-27-07-2026-en) is the executive summary. Risk to DNSSEC-validating resolvers that haven't loaded KSK-2024: **total DNS resolution failure**.

**Recent comms**:
- Aug 26 EMEA Webinar Series — [ICANN engagement-calendar entry](https://www.icann.org/en/engagement-calendar/details/emea-webinar-series-ksk-rollover-2026-2026-08-26) + [ISOC Live recording](https://isoc.live/21842) (password `BmEKv5ue?\`).
- Sep 4–6 ICANN Board LA workshop — agenda included New gTLD 2026 Round; Reveal Day date still expected in mid-Sep window per ICANN's Aug 13 announcement.
- Sep 9 ICANN blog ["Chair's Blog: September Board Workshop Preview"](https://www.icann.org/en/blogs/) — the pre-meeting chair's preview. No Reveal Day date confirmed publicly yet as of Sep 14.

**Critical post-rollover fact for the decentralized-DNS stack**: DNSSEC-validating resolvers are the universal choke point. Any naming system that uses DNSSEC anchoring (ENS DNSSEC import, Namecoin via ncp11, Handshake via resolver trust) inherits the dependency. Decentralized alternatives that don't use DNSSEC at all (GNS, PKARR/PKDNS, Tor .onion, Yggdrasil IPv6, Namecoin's P2P mode, Slashtags) are unaffected. This is the only scheduled global DNS event in 2026.
