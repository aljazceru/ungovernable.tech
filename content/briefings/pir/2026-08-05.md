## Key Research Findings (August 2026)

### The Field Has Diversified Explosively

The PIR research landscape in 2025–2026 is no longer just about raw throughput. The frontier has split into seven active subproblems:

1. **Incremental / dynamic databases** — iSimplePIR (entry-level updates), Conflux (keyword PIR for dynamic data), PIRacle (key-value stores). The static-DB assumption that limited PIR's practicality is being dismantled.

2. **Keyword PIR** — KPIR (USENIX Sec 2025), Conflux (HPCA 2026), ChalametPIR. Practical systems need keyword lookup, not just index-based retrieval. KPIR provides a generic transformation from keyword to index PIR via sparse key-value stores.

3. **Verifiability** — VeriSimplePIR (USENIX Sec 2024, 27 citations), "Verifiable PIR with Small Client Storage" (S&P 2026). Detecting malicious servers that return wrong results. The S&P 2026 follow-up reduces client state from >1 GiB by orders of magnitude and introduces covert vPIR for stateless clients.

4. **Hardware acceleration** — GPIR (GPU, 305×), SmartPIR (CSDs, MICRO 2025), IM-PIR (processing-in-memory). PIR's memory-bound nature makes it ideal for near-data processing architectures.

5. **Communication reduction** — FlashPIR (2.6× over WhisPIR), ZipPIR (no client storage), VIA (S&P 2026), Pirouette (transciphering alternative), InsPIRe, Improved TensorPIR (45.5% reduction).

6. **Preprocessing elimination** — YPIR (silent preprocessing), WhisPIR (stateless), ZipPIR (no client storage at all). Removing the hint download barrier.

7. **Ethereum integration** — The first major platform-level deployment target. Sharded PIR design (ethresear.ch), Kohaku privacy toolkit (Vitalik endorsed), PSE PIR project, RisePIR for key-value workloads.

### Ethereum PIR — The Deployment Signal

The strongest real-world deployment signal for PIR comes from Ethereum:

- **Vitalik Buterin's privacy roadmap** (May 2026): Explicitly calls for PIR + ORAM for wallet privacy. Kohaku toolkit introduced as the implementation vehicle.
- **Sharded PIR Design** (ethresear.ch, Ali Atiia & Keewoo Lee): Comprehensive architecture for pairing Ethereum data slices with optimal PIR schemes. Proposes decoy queries across all shards for uniform privacy.
- **RisePIR**: Keyword PIR for key-value stores using Segmented Cuckoo Filters. Maps directly to `(address → balance)` lookups. Single-threaded prototype achieves 1,500–24,000 ops/s for 32-byte values — squarely in the regime needed for `eth_getBalance`.
- **PSE Project**: Active Ethereum Foundation team building PIR for Ethereum data (pse.dev/blog/ethereum-privacy-pir, June 2025).

### Apple — The Existence Proof

Apple's iOS 18 (2024) ships HE+PIR for Live Caller ID, logo lookup, and content filtering. This is the **largest-scale production deployment** of PIR-class techniques in consumer hardware. It proves the approach works at consumer scale on mobile devices, though Apple's implementation is proprietary and not independently auditable.
