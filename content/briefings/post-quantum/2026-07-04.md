## The Week in Detail

### 1. Microsoft Accelerates PQC Roadmap (July 2026)
Microsoft updated its Secure Future Initiative to explicitly include post-quantum cryptography. Focus areas:
- TLS 1.3 hybrid KEMs (X25519 + ML-KEM-768) in Azure and Microsoft 365 edge.
- Crypto-agility libraries and tooling for internal services.
- Trust chain modernization (code signing, attestation).
- Emphasis on “PQ-ready” supply chain for customers.

This follows Google’s 2029 target and Cloudflare’s full-PQ authentication push. Enterprise tooling (SDKs, monitoring) is now shipping, lowering the barrier for smaller projects.

**Relevance**: Decentralized apps can adopt Microsoft’s open patterns or liboqs integrations without reinventing migration tooling.

### 2. NIST Begins Wiring PQC into PIV Credentials
NIST published initial working drafts integrating:
- ML-DSA for digital signatures on PIV cards.
- ML-KEM for key establishment in authentication flows.

This is the first standardized hardware token transition path. Expect commercial PIV vendors (HID, Gemalto, etc.) to follow with firmware updates in 2026-2027.

**Relevance to Ungovernable.tech**: Hardware-backed identity (YubiKey-style or custom secure elements) for pseudonymous systems can start planning PQ attestation keys. Long-term sealed data or root-of-trust artifacts benefit from early hybrid designs.

### 3. liboqs Ecosystem & Standardization Cleanup
- **0.16.0-rc1** (June 24) testing continues; SPHINCS+ fully deprecated in favor of SLH-DSA.
- oqs-provider updates syncing with OpenSSL 3.5+ native PQC expectations.
- Language bindings (Rust, Go, Python) seeing performance improvements and ACVP test coverage.
- New: Better support for hybrid KEM combinations and constant-time guarantees post-KyberSlash lessons.

Primary source: https://github.com/open-quantum-safe/liboqs (releases page)

### 4. Policy & International Developments
- **OMB M-26-15 Playbook**: Agencies racing to deliver 120-day plans. Prioritization matrix focuses on HVAs and high-impact systems first.
- **DoD PQC Strategy**: Companion document released alongside the EO, emphasizing supply-chain and classified system timelines.
- **G7 Financial Sector**: Roadmap discussions emphasize 2030-2035 window for banks and payment systems.
- **EU/UK Alignment**: Similar accelerated timelines emerging.

**Relevance**: Even purely decentralized projects will eventually interface with regulated entities (exchanges, cloud providers, hardware vendors). Adopting NIST PQC standards now ensures interoperability and future-proofs against “harvest now, decrypt later” adversaries.
