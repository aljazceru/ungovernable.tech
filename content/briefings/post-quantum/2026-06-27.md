This note covers developments since the June 20 update. The dominant story is the **Trump PQC Executive Order** (June 23) imposing hard federal deadlines (2030/2031), the **liboqs 0.16.0-rc1** release (June 24) with SLH-DSA standardization and SPHINCS+ removal, and the **OMB M-26-15 Migration Playbook**. Cloudflare accelerates its full-PQ (incl. authentication) target to 2029. Freedom-tech takeaway: **policy is now forcing the migration that implementers have been warning about; crypto-agility, inventory of long-lived keys, and hybrid defaults are no longer optional for any sovereign or decentralized system.***

- **Trump PQC Executive Order (June 23, 2026)** — Directs federal agencies to appoint PQC transition leads within 30 days. Mandates PQ key migration for “high value assets” and “high impact systems” by **Dec 31, 2030**; PQ digital signatures by end of **2031**. NIST pilot project due by end 2027. Procurement rules for contractors by 2030. Accelerates previous 2035 timelines. Companion quantum innovation EO also signed.

- **liboqs 0.16.0-rc1 (June 24, 2026)** — Removes SPHINCS+ (replaced by SLH-DSA per FIPS 205). Stronger mldsa-native integration. Continues the standardization cleanup.
- **Cloudflare 2029 Full Post-Quantum Target** — Updated roadmap prioritizes post-quantum *authentication* (certificates, signatures) alongside encryption. Cites recent neutral-atom and algorithm advances pulling Q-Day estimates forward.
- **NIST Additional Signatures Round 3** — 9 candidates (FAEST, HAWK, MAYO, MQOM, QR-UOV, SDitH, SNOVA, SQIsign, UOV) continue evaluation (~2-year round).
- **Conferences & Momentum** — ETSI/IQC QSC 2026 (Ottawa, June 16-18); GSA 2026 PQC Summit scheduled for Sept 16.

The freedom-tech takeaway: **Washington has now set an enforceable clock. Decentralized projects must treat 2030 as a hard migration horizon for any system with long-lived keys or authentication (Nostr, Bitcoin, mesh networks, confidential compute attestation). Hybrid KEMs + crypto-agility remain the pragmatic path.**

## The Week in Detail

### 1. Trump PQC Executive Order — “Securing the Nation Against Advanced Cryptographic Attacks” (June 23)

Signed Monday June 23. Key provisions:
- Agencies identify lead PQC officials within **30 days**.
- High-value / high-impact systems: PQ keys by **Dec 31, 2030**; PQ signatures by **Dec 31, 2031**.
- NIST to run migration pilot (complete by end 2027).
- OMB issues detailed guidance within 90 days (M-26-15 playbook already out).
- Federal Acquisition Regulatory Council to impose PQC requirements on contractors by 2030.
- Emphasis on crypto-agility, supply-chain, and shared procurement.

Industry reaction (Cloudflare, QuSecure, Keeper Security, etc.): “Lights a fire,” “makes NIST work enforceable,” “quantum readiness is no longer a future problem.” Funding questions remain (~$7B+ estimated previously).

**Relevance to Ungovernable.tech**: Any project relying on classical signatures or KEMs for identity, software distribution, or long-term sealed data now has a concrete external deadline. Inventory long-lived keys (root CAs, code-signing, Nostr identity keys, Bitcoin multisig) immediately.

### 2. liboqs 0.16.0-rc1 (June 24)

- **SPHINCS+ removed**; fully replaced by SLH-DSA (FIPS 205).
- Continued mldsa-native and mlkem-native integration improvements.
- Part of the broader cleanup aligning with finalized NIST standards.

Projects using liboqs (OpenSSL oqs-provider, OpenSSH, language wrappers) should plan the 0.16 upgrade and drop legacy SPHINCS+ paths.

### 3. Cloudflare Roadmap Update (targeting 2029 full PQ)

Cloudflare now publicly targets **2029** for *complete* post-quantum security, including authentication (not just encryption). 
- Current state: >65% of human traffic already PQ-encrypted.
- Drivers: Recent neutral-atom hardware progress + improved error-correction + faster Shor variants on P-256/RSA.
- Priority shift: Authentication (long-lived keys) is now the urgent frontier because a single forged root or code-signing key is catastrophic once Q-Day arrives.

**Recommendation from Cloudflare (and echoed here)**: 
- Inventory all quantum-vulnerable long-lived keys.
- Deploy hybrid + PQ where possible.
- Plan for disabling classical fallbacks + secret rotation.
- PQ HSTS / Certificate Transparency for downgrade protection.
