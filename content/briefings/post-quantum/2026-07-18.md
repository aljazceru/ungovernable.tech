## The Week in Detail

### 1. liboqs 0.16.0 — Canonical Open-Source PQC Library (July 9, 2026)

The Open Quantum Safe project released liboqs v0.16.0, the first fully stable post-standardization release.

- Full support for FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA), FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA), HQC (selected 2025), with FN-DSA work ongoing.
- SPHINCS+ removed (replaced by SLH-DSA); performance improvements especially for hash-based signatures.
- Ecosystem updates: oqs-provider (OpenSSL 3.x), language bindings (Rust, Go, Python, .NET), PQClean implementations.
- GitHub: https://github.com/open-quantum-safe/liboqs/releases/tag/0.16.0
- Relevance: Now the reference implementation for any Ungovernable.tech stack. Use for TLS/SSH (via oqs-provider), custom protocols (Nostr PQ key exchange, Bitcoin PQ primitives), or attestation in confidential computing. Avoid legacy Kyber/Dilithium parameter sets.

### 2. Executive Order 14412 & Federal Migration Acceleration

On June 22, 2026, President Trump signed **Executive Order 14412**, "Securing the Nation Against Advanced Cryptographic Attacks," accompanied by OMB Memorandum M-26-15.

Key requirements and timelines:
- Agencies must identify PQC migration leads (within 30-90 days of EO).
- Prioritized migration of high-value assets (HVAs) and high-impact systems:
  - Key establishment (encryption): Dec 31, 2030
  - Digital signatures/authentication: Dec 31, 2031
- Federal contractors required to comply with PQC FIPS by end of 2030.
- CISA/NIST to issue CBOM (Cryptographic Bill of Materials) guidance within 270 days.
- Aligns with updated Q-Day estimates (Google Willow chip advances, reduced qubit estimates).

Sources and context:
- White House EO: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/securing-the-nation-against-advanced-cryptographic-attacks/
- Cloudflare analysis: https://blog.cloudflare.com/post-quantum-eo-2026/
- CISA PQC Initiative: https://www.cisa.gov/topics/risk-management/quantum

**Freedom-tech relevance**: While focused on federal systems, the EO creates de-facto industry standards and supply-chain pressure. Decentralized projects should mirror the inventory + migration planning approach to avoid being left behind or vulnerable to "harvest now, decrypt later" on long-lived keys/identities.

### 3. FIPS 140-3 Enforcement & Hardware Migration

NIST CMVP will retire remaining FIPS 140-2 certificates to Historical status on **September 21, 2026**. Only FIPS 140-3 modules will be accepted for new federal procurement.

- Average validation timeline now >500 days.
- Direct impact on PQ-capable HSMs (Thales, Utimaco, AWS CloudHSM, Azure Dedicated HSM) and secure elements.
- CISA guidance on PQC product categories updated earlier in 2026.

This accelerates vendor timelines for ML-KEM/ML-DSA support in hardware roots of trust, critical for confidential computing attestation and key sealing.

### 4. ISO Standardization: Classic McEliece

Post-Quantum (company) announced Classic McEliece as the **first post-quantum cryptography algorithm to achieve global ISO standardization** (July 16, 2026 reports).

- Code-based KEM with strong conservative security profile (long history, no major breaks).
- Complements lattice-based (ML-KEM) and hash-based (SLH-DSA) standards.
- https://industrialcyber.co/news/post-quantums-classic-mceliece-becomes-first-post-quantum-cryptography-algorithm-to-achieve-global-iso-standardization/

Useful as a backup or for high-assurance use cases in freedom-tech (e.g., long-term key encapsulation in decentralized storage or messaging).

### 5. NIST Additional Signatures & PIV Integration

Round 3 of NIST's Additional Digital Signature Standardization (9 candidates: FAEST, HAWK, MAYO, MQOM, QR-UOV, SDitH, SNOVA, SQIsign, UOV) entering 2-year evaluation period.

- Working drafts progressing for PIV (Personal Identity Verification) integration of ML-DSA/ML-KEM.
- Focus on compact signatures for constrained environments (smart cards, IoT, hardware tokens).

Relevant for Web of Trust, Nostr identity, and mesh attestation schemes.

### 6. Blockchain & Cryptocurrency Migration Research

Increased focus on quantum risks to Bitcoin and Ethereum:

- Google researchers (April 2026 onward) warning of accelerated Q-Day timelines; urging immediate migration planning.
- New paper: "Migrating Bitcoin and Ethereum Addresses to the Quantum Blockchain Era" (eprint 2026/352) proposes hybrid classical/PQ authorization for revealed-key assets and STARK-verifiable ownership for hidden keys.
- Ethereum roadmap emphasizes account abstraction advantages for PQ migration (EIP-7701, leanSig XMSS/Poseidon2 experiments).
- Bitcoin discussions around BIP-360 soft forks, dual-address periods, and 76-day full UTXO migration estimates.
- Project Eleven and others predicting Q-Day as early as 2030.

**Ungovernable.tech angle**: [Bitcoin PQ Migration](/kb/bitcoin-post-quantum-migration/) and [Hybrid KEM Migration](/guides/hybrid-kem-migration/) notes should incorporate these strategies. Hybrid schemes (X25519+ML-KEM) remain the pragmatic near-term path for wallets and identity systems. SLH-DSA or stateful hash signatures for long-term cold storage.
