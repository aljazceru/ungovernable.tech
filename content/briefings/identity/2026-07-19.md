## The Week in Detail

### Marmot Protocol & Group Pseudonymity
Marmot (MLS + Nostr + Blossom) continues adoption for decentralized E2E encrypted group messaging using Nostr public keys as stable identity anchors. 

- White Noise client updates (v2026.5.22): iOS push live, markdown support, improved mdk crates. Requires Rust 1.90+ and SQLite; supports MIP-defined group flows (KeyPackage, Welcome, Group Events).
- Marmot spec status: Adopted; MIPs cover protocol surfaces. TypeScript bindings (marmot-ts) in early development. Security audit fixes merged (hash verification, pagination, secret zeroization).
- Ties directly into [Nostr](/kb/nostr-identity/) and group identity contexts; enables shared pseudonymity without central servers.

### Bitchat & Offline Mesh Identity
Bitchat (BLE mesh + Nostr relays) emerges as practical off-grid pseudonymity layer:

- Permissionless, no accounts/internet required for core BLE multi-hop store-and-forward.
- Real deployments: Event coordination (BLE first → Nostr gateway for hybrid reach), geohash channels, presence signals, creator-managed channels with NIP-13 PoW.
- Stacking: Frequently paired with Nostr, Tor, Signal alternatives for journalists/whistleblowers resisting surveillance or EU regs.
- Enhancements: Opt-in mesh-to-Nostr, prekey bundles, transitive verification.

See also [08-Mix-Networks](/pillars/anonymity/) and off-grid discussions.

### VaultChat & Mobile Nostr Identity
New Android entrant: VaultChat

- On-device key generation in Android Keystore (never leaves device).
- NIP-44 v2 encryption, WebRTC calls, PIN/biometric lock, encrypted backups.
- Public security architecture; positions Nostr keys as the core pseudonymous identity for conversations.

### Event, Payment & Marketplace Integrations
- **Satlantis / BTCHEL 2026**: Nostr npubs for event check-ins, attendee social graphs; portable identity across Lightning-integrated apps.
- **Mostro & P2P**: Client-side 12-word seed identities (no KYC/email/ID); NIP-44 anti-spam in Transport v2.
- Wallets (Bitcoin Safe, BuhoGO): Random or NIP-06 derived nsecs, NIP-17 gift-wrapped DMs, compartmentalization.
- Broader: Bring-your-own-Nostr-identity in payments and marketplaces.

### Signers, NIP Evolution & Future-Proofing
- Remote/hardware signing (NIP-46/55) via NostrKey Safari extension and Android support.
- NIP-05 DNS verification in use alongside critiques; NIP-SPACES (Bitcoin PoW naming) as sovereign alternative.
- AI/agent identity: Emerging protocols for verifying AI agents on Nostr-like graphs.
- Post-quantum: Parallel explorations of FIPS 204 (ML-DSA) for future DIDs alongside classical secp256k1.
