## TL;DR — This Week
- **Nostr Compass #30 & Protocol Updates**: Marmot spec adopted across ecosystem (encrypted groups, external signers); Mostro v0.18.0 + Mobile v1.3.0 ships Transport v2 on NIP-44 with anti-spam; Bitchat 1.6.0 adds NIP-13 PoW, opt-in mesh-to-Nostr gateway, prekey bundles.
- **Signer & Mobile Ecosystem**: NostrKey Safari extension (multi-profile, encrypted vault, nsecBunker); Bitcoin Safe uses NIP-17 gift-wrapped DMs with random nsec defaults; CustID beta momentum continues with NFC/QR flows.
- **Wallet & P2P Integrations**: Satlantis auto-provisions Nostr identities + Lightning; Mostro P2P marketplace uses 12-word seed client-side identities ("No registration. No email. No ID selfie"); BuhoGO derives Nostr ID via NIP-06 for cross-wallet compatibility; Bitcoin payments systems allow bring-your-own Nostr identities.
- **NIP & Naming Developments**: NIP-05 DNS verification in use (with some centralization critiques); NIP-SPACES proposed as Bitcoin PoW-anchored sovereign alternative to DNS; NIP-39 profile linking; NIP-17 encrypted DMs prominent in wallets.
- **AI Agent & Broader Identity**: Discussions around agentic AI identity (x401 protocol for verifying AI agents); parallel post-quantum (FIPS 204 ML-DSA) explorations for future-proof sovereign DIDs.
- **Mesh & Discovery**: FIPS v0.4.0 (from prior week) seeing continued integration; myco P2P nsite sharing over mesh.

**Freedom-tech takeaway**: Nostr's pseudonymous keypair model is embedding deeply into Bitcoin/Lightning ecosystems for self-sovereign payments, marketplaces, and social coordination. Hardware/remote signers, seed-phrase determinism (NIP-06), encrypted DM primitives (NIP-17), and mesh extensions reduce custody risks while enabling compartmentalized, revocable-reputation identities. This week's momentum points to practical, ungovernable deployments where identity is portable, verifiable without central authorities, and resilient to both censorship and future quantum threats.

## The Week in Detail

### Nostr Compass #30 & Core Protocol Maturation
Nostr Compass continues weekly tracking of NIP adoption and client/relay hardening. Key highlights from the July 8 newsletter period:

- **Marmot spec adoption**: Marked adopted across 42 files. MDK cuts v0.9.0–v0.9.3 with encrypted group avatars, external signer support, MarmotKit iOS/Android bindings. Strengthens group pseudonymity and shared identity contexts.
  - Ties into [Nostr](/kb/nostr-identity/) and group/MLS primitives.

- **Mostro Transport v2**: Ships on NIP-44 direct messages with anti-spam gates and coexistence window in mostrod v0.18.0 and Mobile v1.3.0. P2P Lightning/Nostr marketplace emphasizes client-side 12-word seed identity generation—no central registration.

- **Bitchat 1.6.0**: Adds NIP-13 proof-of-work to geohash channel messages, opt-in mesh-to-Nostr gateway (one phone uplinks a crowd), prekey bundles, transitive verification, creator-managed channels. Extends pseudonymous mesh identity to local/off-grid scenarios.

Additional: Whitenoise Linux desktop Marmot client; protocol proposals for dedicated event kinds.

NIPs repo activity: Continued discussion and merges around relay management, search (NIP-50), trusted assertions (NIP-85).

### Signer Ecosystem & Key Custody
- **NostrKey Web Extension** (App Store): Safari extension for macOS/iOS managing multiple Nostr identities, signing without exposing nsec, encrypted vault, nsecBunker support, auto-lock, per-site permissions. Private keys never leave device unless backed up.
  - https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nostrkey-web-extension/id6759624317

- **Bitcoin Safe**: Defaults to random nsec (separate from social keys) with NIP-17 gift-wrap encrypted DMs; supports custom relays (opt-in). Emphasizes compartmentalized pseudonymity.

- **CustID** momentum from prior week carries forward as hardware-backed NIP-46 signer with NFC challenges.

- Broader: Emphasis on NIP-06 deterministic derivation from seeds for cross-wallet loading (e.g., BuhoGO).

Key management best practices reiterated across sources: generate locally, immediate backup of nsec, compartmentalization for different personas (main vs. anonymous vs. testing).

### Wallet, Marketplace & Application Integrations
- **Satlantis**: Auto-provisions attendees with Lightning wallet/address + Nostr identity (or bring-your-own), removing setup friction for events/communities.
- **Mostro P2P**: "No registration. No email. No ID selfie." Identities fully client-side via seed phrase.
- **Bitcoin payments/tollbooth systems**: Users bring own Nostr identities; decouples value from identity; supports multiple/borrowed identities; no KYC friction.
- **NIP-05 usage**: Human-readable verification (e.g., sooly@nostrarabia.com) remains popular but faces critique over DNS/ICANN centralization risks.
- **NIP-SPACES**: Emerging Bitcoin PoW-anchored naming protocol as more sovereign alternative to NIP-05/DNS for true censorship resistance.

NIP-39 (Linking Profiles to Other Platforms) and NIP-17 (encrypted DMs) see active implementation in wallets and social tools.

### Proof of Personhood, Reputation & Agentic Identity
- Continued enterprise traction for World ID-style systems alongside academic/social-graph and cryptographic approaches (as covered in overview).
- Emerging **x401 Open Protocol** (Proof): Issuer-neutral verification of human authority behind AI agents; selective disclosure + ZK proofs; submitted to FIDO Alliance agentic auth workgroup. Companion to x402 agent payments. Live MCP server with Claude/ChatGPT.
- Broader agentic AI identity summit discussions (Identiverse 2026 context) highlight shift to non-human/agent identities outnumbering human ones; need for sovereign, on-chain quantum-safe DIDs (tying into FIPS 204 ML-DSA / post-quantum signatures for future-proofing).

### Mesh, Off-Grid & Discovery Extensions
FIPS v0.4.0 (Nym mixnet transport, mDNS, rekeying) integrates with myco for P2P nsite sharing over BLE/L2CAP. Bitchat mesh gateway provides hybrid local-to-Nostr pseudonymity. Supports offline-first, compartmentalized identity use cases.
