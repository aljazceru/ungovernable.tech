## The Week in Detail

### Nostr Web of Trust & Reputation Systems
Nostr WoT infrastructure advances with dedicated tooling for decentralized trust and filtering:
- nostr-wot GitHub organization and nostr-wot.com site provide open-source tools for building, querying, and visualizing trust networks based on social graphs.
- Core mechanism: Social distance from trusted seed pubkeys via follow graphs (NIP-02), augmented by mutes/reports; enables client-side spam filtering and reputation scoring without central servers.
- Use cases: Marketplace reviews, content recommendation, Sybil resistance in governance/airdrop contexts; extends classical PGP WoT concepts to Nostr's broadcast model.
- Related: Discussions on extending WoT for AI agent verification and trusted assertions.

See also [Overview - Web of Trust](/kb/web-of-trust/) and [Proof of Personhood and Sybil Resistance](/kb/proof-of-personhood/).

### Marmot Protocol, Concord & Encrypted Groups
Group pseudonymity via MLS-over-Nostr continues evolving:
- Marmot spec formally adopted; MIPs and MDK library receive updates for signing proofs, capabilities negotiation, state convergence.
- Client shifts: Vector moves group chats to Concord (community protocol); Amethyst implements clean-room Concord support alongside Marmot interop.
- NIP-29 updates clarify group relay migration, forks, and cluster behaviors for better scalability.
- White Noise and Amethyst integrations emphasize iOS/Android bindings, external signers, and larger-group optimizations.
- Ties into [Nostr](/kb/nostr-identity/) and remote signing for compartmentalized group identities.

### Bitchat, Sonar & Mesh Pseudonymity
Offline-first identity layers advance:
- Bitchat enhancements (NIP-13 PoW for channels, prekey bundles, transitive verification, mesh-to-Nostr opt-in gateways) enable robust event coordination and geohash presence without accounts.
- Sonar emerges as cross-platform spin-off from Bitchat, focusing on BLE multi-hop store-and-forward with Nostr fallback.
- Real deployments: Hybrid online/offline at Bitcoin events, journalist tools stacking with Tor/Nostr for surveillance resistance.
- Complements [08-Mix-Networks](/pillars/anonymity/) and off-grid sections.

### NIP Proposals, Portable Identity & Signers
- NIP-46 remote signing fixes (timeout handling); NIP-55 bunker support in Amber.
- New: kind:10011 favorite follow sets for curated trust lists; debates on NIP-47 wallet simplifications and trusted relay assertions.
- Portable keys: Zapstore adds encrypted device key portability with Amber backups and auto-updates; emphasis on "your keys, your identity" across devices/apps.
- Broader: NostrKey extensions, biometric/hardware signers; early post-quantum explorations alongside secp256k1.
