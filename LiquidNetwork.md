- [Confidential Transactions](https://elementsproject.org/features/confidential-transactions) - How Liquid hides transaction amounts using Bulletproofs
- [Bulletproofs paper](https://eprint.iacr.org/2017/1066.pdf) - Bünz et al. (2018) — short proofs for confidential values
- [Greg Maxwell's CT proposal](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=1078201.0) - Original Confidential Transactions concept (2015)
- [Everyday Guide to Confidential Transactions](https://blog.liquid.net/guide-to-confidential-transactions/) - Technical deep-dive on how CT works on Liquid
