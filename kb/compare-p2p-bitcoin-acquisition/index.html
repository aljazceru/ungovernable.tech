Compare: Peer-to-Peer Bitcoin Acquisition

An exchange can avoid identity registration and still expose you through custody, dispute handling or the payment method.

Usable updated 2026-10-25 4 min read
Contents

Summary

Peer-to-peer acquisition replaces an exchange account with a trade against another person. The security mechanism then matters: multisig escrow, a Lightning coordinator and seller reputation put different parties in control. A platform's lack of identity verification does not make the fiat payment anonymous.

Here, KYC means the platform's identity-verification process. A bank, payment provider, trading counterparty or dispute handler may still learn identifying information. The comparison separates that question from control of the bitcoin.

These ratings concern working software and documented workflows. They do not establish that a matching offer exists for a particular currency, amount or payment method.

How the trust models differ

Escrow is not a uniform guarantee. Bisq's multisig trades require both traders' signatures; its mediator cannot release the escrow alone. Hodl Hodl holds one key in a two-of-three arrangement and normally co-signs with a trader. Peach's trading FAQ describes initial seller-and-Peach control and a timelocked recovery route that later gives Peach control. Those are materially different powers.

A coordinator federation is not shared escrow. RoboSats combines listings from multiple coordinators, but the selected coordinator runs the trade and its dispute process. It does not split custody for each trade among the other coordinators.

Private chat does not erase payment records. Peach's privacy FAQ describes end-to-end encryption for ordinary trade payment details, hashes and trade metadata retained on its servers, and disclosure of chat and payment details to the assigned mediator during a dispute. Instant-trade offers additionally share payment details with Peach. Evaluate the actual trade flow, not a blanket claim of anonymity.

Where atomic swaps fit

Atomic swaps exchange one cryptocurrency for another. In the Monero project's explanation, the protocol makes the exchange complete for both sides or leaves each side with its original funds, without a trusted exchange custodian. That property covers the cryptocurrency exchange, not a bank transfer.

For Bitcoin–Monero swaps, eigenwallet's repository provides the maintained software and interface. The existing Monero entry places swaps among its acquisition options. This route requires cryptocurrency to start with; it is not a first purchase from fiat. Swap direction, available counterparties, software and recovery paths must be checked for the implementation being used. Treat it as a more involved route than a fiat marketplace, not a guarantee of anonymity or a substitute for a fiat rail.

Readiness and choosing a route

Usable, with different trust boundaries. Start by checking the payment method you can actually use and the information it exposes. Then decide whether you accept a coordinator, a service holding an escrow key, a reputation-based seller, or the operational demands of the multisig protocol.

Before sending fiat, verify that the required escrow step has completed. Keep the trade token or recovery data until settlement and any dispute are finished. The trade is only one part of the setup: continue with Bitcoin self-custody and consider Silent Payments and CoinJoin for different aspects of on-chain privacy.
